# VEO-01 — SETELAH LAMPU PADAM

## Purpose
Scene 03 — event aftermath.

## Prompt

Create a photorealistic cinematic documentary sequence showing the immediate aftermath of a large cultural event in Sumenep, Madura, East Java, Indonesia.

Spectators slowly leave the venue in small groups, walking naturally toward the exit. Several local food and UMKM vendors begin packing merchandise, folding tables, collecting containers and organizing their belongings. A few empty chairs and small traces of the event remain.

Start with a medium-wide shot facing the stage and audience area. Slowly track sideways as the crowd thins. Transition naturally toward the vendor area. End on a wider composition where the venue feels noticeably quieter and emptier than at the beginning.

Authentic Sumenep/Madurese atmosphere. Indonesian families, young people and local vendors. Modest realistic temporary event infrastructure. Warm practical lights mixed with surrounding night darkness.

The emotional progression is subtle: lively event atmosphere becoming quiet and reflective.

Premium documentary cinematography, realistic low-light exposure, natural motion blur, physically plausible camera movement, 35mm documentary lens look, shallow depth of field only when appropriate.

No logos. No readable signs. No subtitles. No text. No futuristic elements. No dramatic disaster mood. The scene should feel like a real local event ending, not a fictional movie set.

Duration: approximately 8–10 seconds.
Aspect ratio: 16:9.
Photorealistic.

# VEO-04 — CROWD FOR COMPUTER VISION

## Purpose
Scene 08 — source footage for YOLO-style tracking/compositing.

## Prompt

Generate clean photorealistic documentary footage of a large but realistic crowd attending a cultural event in Sumenep, Madura, East Java, Indonesia.

Camera position: elevated viewpoint approximately 3–5 meters above ground, looking diagonally across the crowd. The camera remains stable and moves slowly forward or sideways.

People should walk naturally in different directions, creating clear individual human silhouettes. Maintain enough spacing that individual people can be visually separated. Include adults, teenagers and families. Some people stand still, some walk, some turn, and some look toward the event.

The crowd should move continuously for the entire shot. Avoid people suddenly appearing or disappearing.

Lighting should be consistent and bright enough for computer-vision tracking. Avoid heavy smoke, extreme darkness, severe motion blur or obstructions.

The composition should resemble real CCTV/event documentation footage rather than a cinematic action sequence.

Important: the people must remain visually distinct and anatomically correct throughout the shot. No duplicated bodies, merging people, warped faces or unnatural movement.

Leave enough visual detail for later post-production object-detection bounding boxes to be composited over the footage.

No bounding boxes in the generated footage.
No numbers.
No UI.
No labels.
No text.
No logos.

Duration: approximately 8–10 seconds.
Aspect ratio: 16:9.
Photorealistic.
